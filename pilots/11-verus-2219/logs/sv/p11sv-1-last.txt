Root cause/fix:

Verus erases ghost/spec expressions into synthetic calls to `erased_ghost_value::<Args, T>`. When `T` was uninhabited, Rust MIR’s `lint_and_remove_uninhabited` treated that synthetic call like a genuinely diverging call and removed its continuation edge. That made reachable post-call code invisible to MIR borrow/linearity checking. The fix keeps continuation edges for Verus’s synthetic `erased_ghost_value` calls by detecting the MIR call operand’s `FnDef` and skipping uninhabited-edge pruning only for that synthetic function. Real diverging calls still prune normally.
